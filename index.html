Who we are

An engineering design team that ships hardware every single year.

QVEX has represented Queen's in the VEX U division since 2019. VEX U is the university tier of VEX Robotics: two robots per team, a fresh game each September, and a deadline that does not move — the VEX Robotics World Championship in April.

That constraint is the whole point. In one academic year a member can take a mechanism from a sketch, through CAD, through the machine shop and 3D printers, onto a real field in front of real judges — and then find out in about fifteen seconds whether the design survives contact with an opponent.

We pull from Smith Engineering, Computing, and the Smith School of Business, and we're recognized by both the Engineering Society and the AMS. Mechanical, electrical, software, and the business side of the operation all sit in the same room, which is the closest thing to a real product team most of us will see before our first internship.

Sub-teams

Four ways in. Pick the one you want to get good at.

Members join one sub-team and are free to cross over. You do not need to arrive knowing any of this — most people learn it on the team.

Mechanical

Design & Manufacturing

Drivetrains, intakes, scoring mechanisms and everything that has to survive being shoved by another robot.

  • CAD and design reviews
  • Machining, lathe and mill work
  • 3D printing and rapid iteration

Electrical

Power & Sensing

Wiring harnesses, motor and battery budgets, and the sensors the autonomous routines depend on.

  • Harness layout and strain relief
  • Sensor selection and mounting
  • Debugging faults at competition

Software

Autonomy & Controls

Driver controls, odometry, motion profiling, and the autonomous routines that score before a human touches a stick.

  • C++ on the VEX V5 brain
  • Odometry and path following
  • PID and motion tuning

Operations

Business & Media

The team runs on funding, logistics and a reputation. This is where that gets built.

  • Sponsorship and finance
  • Graphic design and social
  • Travel and competition logistics

2025–26 season

Push Back.

Every VEX season is a new game revealed in the spring, and every team on earth starts from the same rulebook on the same day.

The game

Two alliances compete on a 12 × 12 ft field to score 88 blocks into long goals and centre goals, hold territory in the control zones, and park before the buzzer. Matches open with an autonomous period — no drivers, no excuses — before handing over to human control.

  • FIELD 12 ft × 12 ft, 6 × 6 foam tiles
  • SCORING 88 blocks · long goals · centre goals
  • ZONES Control zones and an endgame park
  • VEX U Two robots per team, per match
